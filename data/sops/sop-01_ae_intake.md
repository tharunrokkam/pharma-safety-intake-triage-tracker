# SOP-01 Suspected Adverse Event Intake (Fictional Training SOP)

Fictional procedure for the training prototype. Not a real pharmacovigilance procedure.

## 1. Purpose
Defines how intake operators capture a suspected adverse event (AE) report received by email or telephone so that the record is complete, traceable and free of personal identifiers.

## 2. Mandatory Fields
Every suspected adverse event record must capture the product name, the batch or lot number in the format LOT-12345, the date received, the reporter type, a detailed description, the event description and the event onset date. The event onset date cannot be later than the date the report was received. Missing mandatory fields block submission and the operator must obtain the information from the reporter.

## 3. Data Minimisation
Operators must never record patient names, initials, dates of birth, contact details, addresses or medical record numbers. Describe the event in neutral terms, for example "reporter described difficulty breathing (dyspnea) shortly after use" rather than identifying the person involved.

## 4. Terminology Hints
Colloquial reporter wording should be captured verbatim and may be paired with a standard term for searchability: difficulty breathing or shortness of breath (dyspnea), hives (urticaria), severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis), feeling faint (syncope), stomach upset (nausea).

## 5. Scope Limit
Intake staff do not assess causality, give medical advice or decide whether a case is reportable to a regulator. Those decisions are made by qualified pharmacovigilance professionals in the enterprise safety system.

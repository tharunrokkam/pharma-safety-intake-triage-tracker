# SOP-06 Data Privacy and Synthetic Data Rules (Fictional Training SOP)

Fictional procedure for the training prototype.

## 1. Synthetic Data Only
The tracker is a training prototype. Only fictional products such as Fictivex, Placebrix and Nullamycin and fictional lot numbers may be entered. Real patient information is never permitted.

## 2. Prohibited Content
Do not enter names, initials, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, street addresses, national identifiers or medical record numbers. The intake form rejects text that appears to contain such identifiers.

## 3. Pseudonymous Actors
Users act under pseudonymous codes such as OP-01 for operators and REV-02 for reviewers. The audit trail records these codes, actions and timestamps but never the free-text descriptions of a report.
